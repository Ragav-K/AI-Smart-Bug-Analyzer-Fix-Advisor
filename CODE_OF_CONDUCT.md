# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project
community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
ability.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate with empathy and respect.
- Give constructive, technically focused feedback.
- Accept correction gracefully.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.
- Focus on what is best for users and the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Publishing another person's private information
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or bad-faith participation
- Sharing credentials, private reports, or exploit details publicly

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
method listed on the GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept
confidential where reasonably possible. Project maintainers may edit or remove
content, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary.
